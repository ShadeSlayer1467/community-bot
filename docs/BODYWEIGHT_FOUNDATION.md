# Bodyweight Foundation

An active personal program using the existing Workout features. Schedule and guidance are also stored in the program's prescription notes, since the current model has no program-level notes field.

| Week | Monday | Wednesday | Friday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strength A | Strength B | Strength A |
| 2 | Strength B | Strength A | Strength B |

Repeat this alternating order. Choose each workout manually; there is no automatic scheduling or rotation.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Strength B | Sets | Rep range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Bird Dog | 3 | 6–10 | Reps per side |
| Single-Leg Calf Raise | 2 | 10–20 | Reps per leg |

Generally stop with roughly 2–3 good reps available while rebuilding consistency and movement quality. Use controlled repetitions rather than chasing ugly reps. Rest roughly 90–180 seconds on larger movements and 60–120 seconds on smaller/core movements as needed.

Rep ranges are progression ranges, not targets to exceed indefinitely. When the top becomes consistently easy with good form, use the existing progression tools to increase difficulty or choose a harder variation. Recommendations remain optional; keep the current movement if technique does not justify advancing.

Configured chains:

- Chair Squat → **Bodyweight Squat** → Split Squat → Bulgarian Split Squat
- Incline Push-Up → **Push-Up** → Feet-Elevated Push-Up → Deficit Push-Up → Weighted Push-Up
- **Glute Bridge** → Single-Leg Glute Bridge → Weighted Glute Bridge
- **Pike Push-Up** → Feet-Elevated Pike Push-Up → Assisted Handstand Push-Up → Handstand Push-Up

The remaining prescriptions use existing manual progression. Strength B's Single-Leg Glute Bridge stays manual. Weighted variations require you to supply actual resistance; no added loads are invented. Recent performances are notes only, not imported workout history.

This program was added as personal data, without extending the starter seed architecture. Existing exercises, programs, sessions and history are preserved. Strength A is saved as the initial next workout only if no next selection already exists.
